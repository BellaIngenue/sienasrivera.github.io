BlueSkies Minecraft Server
======

[OG WEBSITE](https://sites.google.com/view/blueskiesmc/home)

BlueSkies Minecraft Server is a Minecraft Survival server that I founded and owned. Having started this project in June 2020, she sadly had to close it in mid 2023. She has worked on a multitude of aspects from the: Developer Side (Coding and working with MySQL Databases), to Art Side (Creating a Custom Website and Custom Art), to the Communication and Moderation of the Server itself. She also created a BlueSkies Podcast and Youtube Channel as well.
* This project has been an incredible use of her skills, and she has loved meeting others through the experience. The project has overall, been incredibly successful and has made her friends, and great times.
